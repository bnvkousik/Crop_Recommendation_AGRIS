The seven signals

What the model actually reads

Every prediction is built from the same seven measurements — each pulling its own weight in the forest's decisions.

🟢

Nitrogen (N)

Drives leafy, vegetative growth — measured in kg/ha from a soil test.

🟠

Phosphorus (P)

Supports root development and flowering, also in kg/ha.

🟡

Potassium (K)

Builds disease resistance and overall plant vigor.

🌡️

Temperature

Average field temperature in °C across the growing window.

💧

Humidity

Relative air humidity (%) — critical for tropical vs. arid crops.

🧪

Soil pH

Acidity or alkalinity of the soil, typically between 3.5 and 10.

🌧️

Rainfall

Total seasonal rainfall in millimetres.

🌱

Random Forest

25 trees vote together, reducing the risk of any single tree overfitting.
